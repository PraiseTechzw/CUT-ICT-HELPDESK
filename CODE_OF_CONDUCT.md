# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a respectful, practical, and safe place for collaboration. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and professionalism
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people
- welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience
- protect privacy and avoid sharing real institutional or personal data
- acknowledge uncertainty and distinguish prototype assumptions from approved CUT policy

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of credentials or personal data, and unauthorized testing of institutional systems are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's configured GitHub contact. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
